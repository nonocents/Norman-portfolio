About

Software and design, from the same person.

I'm a solo full-stack developer and graphic designer working out of Cagayan de Oro, Philippines. I studied Information Technology at USTP, and most of my time now goes into building software for the Philippine Crop Insurance Corporation — offline-first mobile apps, real-time dashboards, and government forms that print exactly the way they're supposed to.

Alongside that, I take on freelance web and brand design work — most recently for local restaurants building their first real online presence.

I work alone, end to end: design, front end, back end, and deployment. Fewer handoffs, fewer things lost in translation, and one person who's actually accountable for the result.
